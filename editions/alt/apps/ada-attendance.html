ADA attendance report

Days enrolled and days present, per student, per month — the two columns apportionment is calculated from. A student who enrolled on the 14th is counted from the 14th, not from the first of the month, so mid-year enrollment does not quietly sink the rate.

Present counts present, tardy, and remote (independent-study contact days). Absent and excused both count as not present — an excused absence is still a day your school is not funded for. How your state actually apportions independent study varies; check your own rules before you file.
